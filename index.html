About Dr Robertson

Specialist in women's health

Dr Pippa Robertson is a highly skilled gynaecologist with a special interest in minimally invasive laparoscopic surgery. She completed her medical training at Newcastle University, graduating with distinction, before undertaking specialist training in obstetrics and gynaecology at King Edward Memorial Hospital.

Dr Robertson has held various leadership positions within the hospital system, including Chief Senior Registrar and Registrar, demonstrating her commitment to both clinical excellence and medical leadership. She has pursued additional training in advanced laparoscopic surgery, allowing her to offer patients state-of-the-art, minimally invasive treatment options.

Passionate about research and innovation, Dr Robertson is actively involved in clinical studies, including her work with the EndoOrigins Biobank, which aims to advance the understanding and treatment of endometriosis.

Dr Robertson is dedicated to providing patient-centred, evidence-based care, ensuring that women receive the best possible treatment tailored to their individual needs.

Philosophy & Approach

Evidence-based. Patient-centred.

01

Take symptoms seriously

Endometriosis is historically underdiagnosed. Every consultation begins with a thorough, unhurried review of your history — your symptoms are believed, not minimised.

02

Accurate, targeted investigation

Where required, specialist imaging and diagnostic laparoscopy provide clarity before any treatment decision is made.

03

Individualised treatment planning

Treatment plans are built around your specific diagnosis, life circumstances, and goals — whether that's fertility, pain relief, or quality of life.

04

Minimally invasive when possible

Advanced laparoscopic techniques are prioritised over open surgery wherever appropriate, reducing recovery time and surgical risk.

05

Continuity of care

Follow-up and ongoing management are core to the practice. Endometriosis is a chronic condition that benefits from long-term specialist oversight.

Referral Information

How to refer

Dr Robertson accepts referrals from GPs and other specialists. To ensure timely triage and scheduling, please include the following information with your referral.

Required with all referrals

  • Date of referral
  • Referring GP's name, address, provider number, and HealthLink ID
  • Patient's full name, address, date of birth, and contact number
  • Reason for referral

If available, also include

  • Patient's Medicare number, private health fund details, and email address
Referrals are reviewed and triaged before an appointment is scheduled. Once triaged, our staff will notify the patient when to contact the clinic to book.
Please note: Dr Robertson operates as a private practice and is unable to offer appointments to patients without private health insurance.
1

Obtain a GP referral

Ask your GP to prepare a referral with the required details above. Referrals can be sent to Hollywood Gynaecology via HealthLink ID: kkarthig

2

Referral triage

The clinical team reviews all referrals and prioritises based on clinical urgency. You will be contacted once triage is complete.

3

Schedule your appointment

Once notified, contact the clinic to book your initial consultation at a time that suits you.

4

Initial consultation

A thorough first appointment to review your history, discuss your concerns, and determine next steps together.
